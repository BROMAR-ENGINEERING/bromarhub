Site Overview

Key site safety and location information

Permit to Work

As contractors, we must sign onto the weekly "Work Permit" before commencing any tasks on site. This applies to all planned maintenance work and call outs — no exceptions.

The permit book is located in the Maintenance Office / Electrical Container and runs for the full week. A single permit covers the week's work, so each worker only needs to sign on once for the week — but every worker on site must sign on.

The process is straightforward. If you're unsure how to complete it, refer to a previously completed permit (for example, those filled out by Mike) as a guide.

At the end of the week, the permit must be signed off / closed out to mark completion. If a new week begins and work continues, a fresh permit is started.

Quick summary:

  • Sign on before starting any work — including call outs.
  • Permit book lives in the Maintenance Office / Electrical Container.
  • One permit per week; every worker signs on.
  • Sign off / close the permit at the end of the week.
  • Use Mike's completed permits as a reference if needed.
SWMS

A SWMS needs to be sent in weekly to accompany our Permit to Work.

There is a custom Tyrecycle maintenance template available that can be modified, with the option to add additional steps and hazards as required.

Please note this template covers basic maintenance works only. You are encouraged to add further steps and hazards to suit other tasks being carried out.

Tyrecycle also has their own Take 5 notebooks. We are encouraged to fill these out for at least one job a day. Plenty of books are available in the Maintenance Office / Electrical Container.

LOTOTO

Lock Out — Tag Out — Try Out

Tyrecycle has increased its safety requirements for maintenance work and entry into fortress-guarded areas. The following process must be followed whenever entering a fortress area.

When entering a fortress area:

  • Attach a RED personal lock to the fortress key lock box.
  • Sign onto the LOTO register located at each area — and sign out at completion when removing your lock.
  • A Tyrecycle "LOTO Officer" must place a YELLOW lock on the system before entry.
Most maintenance personnel and supervisors are authorised LOTO officers. Mike can assist if you don't have a lock, or if you'd like to know more about the process.
