🔒 How we handle your information
Your details are sent directly into Bromar's secure admin system — not by email. We follow the Australian Privacy Principles (Privacy Act 1988) and use your information only for managing your employment.
  • Banking and medical information is stored separately with admin-only access.
  • Every view of your record is logged for audit purposes.
  • Sensitive sections are not saved on this device — closing the page clears them.
